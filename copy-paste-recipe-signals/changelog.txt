Version: 1.9.0
Date: 2026-09-27
  Features:
    - When copying several item signals into a requester chest, a picker now lets you paste either
      ALL item signals at once or just one specific item (chosen by its in-game icon)
    - Picking a single item replaces the chest's requests with just that item; the ALL button pastes
      every item signal as before
    - New per-player setting "Ask which items to paste" to turn the picker off and always paste all items
  Bugfixes:
    - Copy/paste now also works with ghost (not-yet-built) entities such as combinators: ghosts are
      resolved to the entity they will become, so their signals can be copied and pasted like the
      built entity. Unsupported ghost operations degrade gracefully instead of failing (a ghost
      requester chest cannot take item requests yet)

Version: 1.8.5
Date: 2026-09-26
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed crash when showing an informational popup after a paste (LuaEntity has localised_name,
      not local_name, and there is no localization global in Factorio 2.0)
    - Fixed pasting to requester chests: use get_requester_point() defensively and handle the manual
      logistic section correctly (filters_count is the section capacity)
    - Buffer chests now accept pasted item requests too; provider/storage chests still show a
      helpful popup instead of crashing
    - Modded requester chests (e.g. Bots Bots Bots' Simple Requester Chest) are now valid paste
      targets (logistic containers get additional_pastable_entities, deduplicated)
    - Fixed decider combinator paste for Factorio 2.0 (per-index conditions/outputs API)
    - Circuit-condition paste no longer writes the enable flag to control behaviors that lack it
    - Unexpected copy/paste errors are now logged and shown as flying text instead of crashing the game
